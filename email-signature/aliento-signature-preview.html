What ships: the email-safe files carry inline light styles plus a @media (prefers-color-scheme: dark) block and an Outlook.com [data-ogsc] fallback. A client that strips <style> shows the light card, which is correct — not a broken one.

Dark is a palette, not an inversion: sage-900 #1E2E24 surface, cream-50 #FEFCF8 name at 13.93:1, sage-200 #B8D5C4 links at 9.07:1. Images swap to their light-ink variants, because a dark-ink signature on a dark card is invisible.
